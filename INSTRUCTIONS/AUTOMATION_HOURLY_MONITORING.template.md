## Prompt

Strategy audit policy STRATEGY-GOVERNANCE-20260911:
Read docs/strategy-governance.md with the complete private rulebook. This is adopted agent-review policy and not registry migration or authority to trade. The zero-active-SELL false-green gate is implemented in the local runtime and validators, but the running MCP bridge must expose the current contract before relying on those fields; the remaining strategy-governance implementation backlog stays open. Keep every existing safety, exact-authorization, named-asset and validator gate.

Report four independent results: accounting integrity, decision freshness, execution lifecycle and economic exposure. A complete dormant ladder can satisfy governed planning while execution is NOT_PLACED and exposure stays UNDERWEIGHT. Review all below-SEK-25,000 positions, including the 20,000-25,000 borderline band; retain the existing below-20,000 structural gate. Plans, accepted orders, exceptions and waits do not restore exposure. Only actual fills or an explicit target change resolve the revised allocation; preserve original targets and lot history. Missing approved targets are TARGET_UNSET. No universal minimum purchase is implied.

Classify campaigns as strategic core (normally 3-12 months or a deliberately longer horizon), tactical participation (2-12 weeks), or event/special situation (days to about 6 weeks). These are review horizons, not forced exits. Do not invent drawdown, cash/reserve mandates or campaign baselines. Freeze the existing 75% quality / 50% high-beta retained-floor convention against the approved campaign baseline, rounded upward to absolute core Antal. Preserve campaign ID, target and cumulative sale allowance; do not lower the floor after sales or reset allowance after fills. Aggregate sequential SELLs, never assume OCO, and retain the five-session cycle brake.

Classify entries as PULLBACK, CONTINUATION, REVERSAL or TAIL_RESIDUAL. Use stock-specific materially sized stages, not generic fractions or percentage vectors. Keep actual pullbacks in the positive-drop schema; unsupported continuation representation is MACHINE_ENFORCEMENT_PENDING, never a forged drop. LADDER_DORMANT means unplaced. An actual conditional order is AWAITING_TRIGGER even when remote; a price-only trigger does not wait for analytical event/volume/thesis gates. Report any mismatch and keep practical participation separate from remote tail coverage.

A lower fixed BUY can fill while a SELL stop-limit remains unfilled. Independently active pre-sale BUYs must be funded and stress-tested alongside the full unsold holding without assumed sale proceeds, and kept separate from sold-lot allocation. Otherwise wait for authenticated sale fills and disclose latency/rebound risk. Price separation is not fill dependency; claim broker-enforced dependency only when current MCP capability, preview and readback prove it. Optional intact-thesis harvesting needs a funded disposition and pauses while prior debt grows or remains stranded. Risk-critical exits do not wait for a recovery plan, but retain all exact authority and broker safety gates. Recovered units default to core; no automatic matching SELL.

Track same-account/same-instrument unfilled recovery in Antal separately from allocated capital-disposition debt in SEK. Active orders may reserve accounting coverage, never economic-restoration credit. A completed rotation needs an explicit source-target disposition; never subtract unlike ticker quantities or cross-account fills. Preserve FX/cost basis, retained cash surplus, source allocation parity and mark-to-market target shortfalls separately.

Require decision_at, evidence_as_of, next_review_at, valid_until, exact scope, source IDs, materiality and supersedes for changed decisions. Check elapsed deadlines and semantic contradictions independently of stale_plan_count; never self-renew terminal decisions. Distinguish DRAFT, VALIDATED, SUBMITTED, ACCEPTED, PARTIALLY_FILLED, FILLED, REJECTED, EXPIRED, CANCELLED and UNKNOWN. Preserve partial fills after cancellation/expiry; journal synchronous no-ID rejections; resolve ambiguous state by readback, never blind retry. Zero raw failed rows does not establish that submissions succeeded.

Keep last successful refresh, source/session timestamps, missed transitions and the next actual check explicit. Do not claim continuous surveillance between invocations. Higher-frequency lifecycle/changed-row monitoring is not activated by this prompt; do not change schedules or create duplicate jobs. Reduced-scope checks cannot satisfy the full governance/streak gate. Continue unaffected read-only scopes while retaining applicable account-wide safety/capacity blockers.

Assess matched-window flow-adjusted total return, defensible benchmarks, drawdown, costs, turnover, time below target and actual recovery fills. Label missing history and modeled inputs. Sale cash is not profit and a governance streak is not investment success. Validate ideas only in a decision-only shadow log, not automatic paper/live orders. Stay quiet on unchanged routine state; notify only material change, failure, completion or required user action.

Run a concise read-only Avanza monitoring pass across the exact user-authorized account scopes, verified independently in authenticated native MCP sessions. Do not expand scope to other visible accounts or change the runtime cadence from this reference. First read `INSTRUCTIONS/INSTRUCTIONS.md`, `INSTRUCTIONS/MEMORY.md`, and `INSTRUCTIONS/TRACKER_STATE.md` if they exist. If a current instrument strategy master is named by the tracker or priority ledger, read it before analysis. Memory contains lessons and strategy updates only, not live portfolio state. Tracker state is a working snapshot that must be refreshed from MCP before action.

Start by verifying Avanza MCP health/status, available capabilities/tools, TradingView/auth/session status if exposed, read/write state, paper trading state, live mutation state, account list, and all active tenant sessions. Use the multi-account workflow: call `avanza_sessions` and `avanza_accounts`, then read each relevant account explicitly by `tenant_session_id` and `account_id`. Do not rely on the selected/default account except as fallback context. Do not assume any account ID, account name, holding, stop-loss ID, order ID, or prior account-specific state.

Avanza U.S. extended-hours gate, effective 2026-09-08: classify the announced Stockholm-local windows as `PRE_MARKET 11:00-15:30`, `REGULAR 15:30-22:00`, and `AFTER_HOURS 22:00-22:30`, subject to current MCP venue/calendar/DST verification. Extended-hours evidence does not complete regular-session gates. Stop-loss and Limit-on-Close are unavailable outside the regular session; pre-market orders can roll into regular trading, while unfilled after-hours orders are cancelled at `22:30`. The order ticket can show realtime extended-hours prices while the portfolio overview remains on the official close. Require explicit session opt-in, hard limit, exact quantity, current spread/depth, and full-friction review. For each account, refresh portfolio/positions, active stop-losses, open/ongoing orders, paper orders if available, recent transactions, buying power/cash where exposed, realtime quotes, and relevant market/news/earnings/crypto-linked context. Keep output compact and avoid raw tool dumps.

Event-first earnings and catalyst gate: scan every holding for same-day, after-close, before-open, next-session, and near-term catalysts. For every upcoming or recent report, unusual move, or materially news-sensitive holding, assess the entity before proposing trim/add/protection: exact report timing; prior guidance versus consensus; estimate revisions; analyst target/rating changes; prior-quarter beat/miss and guide quality; product/customer/partnership announcements; management pre-signals; peer and sector read-throughs; macro/geopolitical/FX/oil/rates sensitivity; TradingView trend, relative strength, extension, and volume; short interest/options-implied move where available. Classify as bullish pre-position, mixed hold, overextended protect/harvest, or bearish reduce. Do not default to merely protecting or trimming; strong clue clusters can justify staged pre-positioning before the event.

Critical gap-risk rule: never describe a holding as protected through earnings, after-hours, pre-market, halted-market, geopolitical shock, or other binary catalyst solely because sell stop-losses exist. Avanza stop-losses are trigger-based controls, not guaranteed fills. Tight `Kurs` values can avoid bad normal-session fills but can fail, remain unfilled, or show `ERROR` if price gaps through trigger/order price. Treat any stop-loss status `ERROR` as unprotected for that slice until verified and replaced/deleted in a user-authorized live thread.

Tracker and buy-back review: one-share/tiny residual positions are decision markers, not automatic buy-back authority. Protection/governance completeness is separate from economic exposure adequacy; when intent is increase/rebuild, a formally valid marker-only row remains underweight until actual fills reach the approved target or an explicit target change resolves the revised allocation. A funded path or time-boxed gate resolves scheduling, not exposure. If a sell stop has triggered or a manual sale occurred, classify the forward choice as rebuild, partial participation, deep residual, hold current exposure, or thesis-broken/no-reentry. Historical loss and missed upside are diagnostic evidence only. The old sale price is a reference, not a ceiling: a hard-capped participation tranche may be above it when current thesis, momentum/structure, event, liquidity, risk, concentration, friction, duplicate, and capacity gates pass; quantify the percentage premium and pair it with any calibrated pullback/deeper residual.

Recovery reachability gate: audit every active BUY row. A deep residual cannot be the only live recovery path and cannot count as practical sold-slice repair. Treat fixed BUYs more than `15%` below current market and `FOLLOW_DOWNWARDS` triggers wider than `4%` as fail-closed review issues, while still calibrating exact replacements from instrument volatility, support, event state, spread, risk, and friction. If no reachable entry passes, record a dormant event/reversal gate rather than preserving an apocalyptic row as fake coverage.

Whole-position and stop review per account: run `avanza_position_strategy_audit` after the live refresh and require every tracked account/orderbook to be `RECORDED` with zero holding, active-stop, regular-open-order, missing-plan, stale-plan, or registry drift. A fill or approved order change creates a review event and must not be silently rebaselined. Run `avanza_stoploss_strategy_audit` and require every active row to have `strategy_metadata_status=RECORDED`; missing, stale-mismatch, or unavailable metadata blocks a clean result and any mutation for that row. Then check `ERROR` rows, stop volume exceeding current holding or the exact approved tactical/profit-harvest `Antal`, retained-core violations, duplicate/conflicting stops, stale validity dates, missing stop-loss/order-book identifiers, stops that can sell below entry when intended as profit protection, too-tight noise-prone stops, too-wide crash-only stops, and open buy orders whose limit/trigger no longer matches the thesis. Call SELL-coverage helpers with exact strategy-classified targets for actionable rows; default/current-active-baseline output must not convert a mechanical full-holding diagnostic into a missing core SELL stop. `Holding - 1` is only a mechanical marker-preserving maximum, never the strategic SELL target. Retain the absolute campaign floor derived from the approved baseline (75% quality / 50% high-beta convention unless an explicit named decision overrides it), never a newly reduced denominator. Recovered/current shares default to core and receive no automatic SELL. Both local strategy registries are evidence, never trade authorization.

Broker SELL protection result: keep position-protection classification completeness separate from actual broker protection. Per account, report active SELL row count plus eligible, broker-covered, and review-required position counts. Per orderbook, report exact held/protected `Antal`, coverage state, and every active SELL row's exact `Antal`. Never sum units across unlike instruments as coverage proof. Registry `governance_complete`, `protection_complete`, `VALID`, or `stale_plan_count=0` output never substitutes for actual SELL coverage; exceptions do not make a position broker-covered and contribute zero protected `Antal` for that orderbook. If an account has zero active SELL rows while any material stop-eligible orderbook has held `Antal > 1`, record `BROKER_SELL_PROTECTION_ABSENT / PROTECTION_REVIEW_REQUIRED` and block clean, fixed, protected, and complete claims. Missing, generic, or elapsed evidence remains review-required. This is a decision gate, not authority for blanket stops; preserve campaign floors, debt brake, exact live approval and named-asset rules.

Independent instrument strategy review: require every held instrument to have a named catalyst, current decision, add gate, sell/exit gate, thesis invalidation, risk-budget rule, factor/theme context, friction rule, loss-recovery rule, and next review. Generic “keep/monitor/review” recommendations are incomplete and block a clean result. Refresh official evidence and live market/account state when a gate is due; the strategy record is audit context, never mutation authority.

Active-order implementation consistency: when an exact pending-order implementation ledger exists, treat it as the active-row source for exact stop IDs, sides, counts, statuses, and modeled notional. Require every recovery, factor, capacity, and displacement artifact to use the same live-source timestamp and reconcile to that ledger. A stale estimated-order source, count/notional mismatch, or revived older keep/replace/cancel decision blocks a clean result. The ledger never authorizes mutation.

Open order monitoring: for every open buy order, verify current quote, day range/pre-market context if available, validity date, order size versus intended exposure, and whether the order is stale, too far below market, too aggressive, or conflicts with current capacity. Track same-account/same-instrument unfilled recovery in Antal separately from capital-disposition debt in SEK. Active orders may reserve accounting coverage, but only exact fills reduce unfilled recovery. Keep FX/cost basis, retained cash and mark-to-market target gaps separate. Crypto-linked tracker monitoring: review active sell stops, buy-back orders, recent transactions, quote freshness, and whether sell/buy stops conflict. Do not cancel protection simply because the asset bounced; compare thesis, support/resistance, flows, rates, geopolitical/oil risk, and relevant market reopen effects.
